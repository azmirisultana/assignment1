Pillars of "Bangladesh 2.0"

Innovation

Innovation drives the journey toward a smarter and more efficient future.

Sustainability

Infrastructure

Empowerment

The Vision of Future

Bangladesh 2.0 aims to redefine the nation’s trajectory by embracing cutiing-edge technology, promoting inclusive growth and ensuring sustainability. With initiatives in renewable energy, digital transformation, and robust infrastructure, the country is set to become a global model of development.

Digital Transformation

Creating a nationwide digital ecosystem to enhance connectivity and access to services for all citizens.

Green Energy

Investments in solar and wind energy to ensure a sustainable and eco-friendly future.

Modern Infrastructure

Building state-of-the-art infrastructure to support economic growth and global competitiveness.
